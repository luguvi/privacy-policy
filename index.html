Luguvi Team Privacy Policy

This Luguvi Team Privacy Policy explains the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide a better user experience:

  • Email & Password: Used for app authentication and secure access.
  • Device Timezone: Used to convert match event dates and times to your local timezone.
  • Mobile Network Information: Used to measure and improve internet connectivity performance within the app.

2. Data Security

We take data security seriously. All data transmission and storage methods are secured, and we follow industry-standard encryption practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third-party services. Your information remains confidential and is used solely for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain user accounts and related information for as long as you actively use our app. Accounts that have not logged in for a period of six (6) months will be automatically deleted, along with all associated data.

5. User Consent

By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
